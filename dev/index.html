FIRST TO 6 WINS · PRACTICE MEDIUM

Turn the shared Core your color and reach 6 or more to win Practice Medium (or 5 in Practice Easy). You are Blue. Your opponent is Red.

ON YOUR TURN

ITIRA! (firing): Choose a Cell Card, then tap ITIRA!. Its number removes enemy cells first. Any amount left adds cells in your color. You draw a replacement card.

Example: The Core is Red 2. You use ITIRA! with +3. Two cancel the Red cells, and the remaining one makes it Blue 1.

Protect: Use your turn to block your opponent's next shot completely. You start with 3 Protects. You also draw a bonus card: keep it in a normal card slot or turn it into Cell Pieces.

The strategy: Push toward 6, stop your opponent, or protect your lead. Using ITIRA! or activating Protect ends your turn.

Cell Pieces & power-ups

Cell Pieces are also called PP. Earn 1 piece for each enemy cell your shot removes. Adding your own cells or hitting a Protect earns none.

Convert a card: Tap its corner arrow to turn a normal Cell Card into pieces equal to its number. The slot stays empty until you fill it with a Protect bonus card. Only normal Cell Cards can be converted.

Bonus cards: If you replace a card, the old card becomes pieces. If you discard the bonus card, that bonus becomes pieces instead.

Gold +4: Spend 10 pieces to use ITIRA! with a +4 shot. This ends your turn.

Refill Protect: Spend 12 pieces to restore one stored Protect, up to 3. You still need to activate it to block a shot.

Converting cards and refilling Protect do not end your turn.

Practice targets & card chances

Practice Easy: 5. Practice Medium: 6. Reaching or passing the target in your color wins. Online Classic is planned, not yet available.

Normal draws: +1 28%, +2 34%, +3 33%, +4 5%.

UPDATE

What's changing in TIRA!

CORBIT → TIRA!

TIRA! is the current name of the game previously called CORBIT. Earlier names included Cell Core and Race to Core. Older links, files, or screenshots may still say CORBIT; they refer to the same game, not a different title.

In-game currency · Cell Pieces

Earlier terms such as Bits or Pieces can cause confusion. Cell Pieces are match resources, not real-money currency. Earn 1 for each enemy Core cell removed by a shot, or convert a normal Cell Card into Pieces equal to its number. Converted slots remain empty until filled with a Protect bonus card.

Spend 10 Pieces to fire Gold +4 (five-player-turn cooldown), or 12 Pieces to restore one Protect, up to three. These are separate from any future account economy.

Goal 5 → 6

First-to-5 can end too quickly and leave little opportunity to recover. 6 remains the standard practice target for more counterplay; 5 moves to Easy Practice. Final competitive balance is still subject to testing.

Custom · Practice

Go to Play → Custom → Practice. Easy is first to 5. Medium uses the previous Classic prototype at first to 6. Other practice difficulties are not listed in this update.
