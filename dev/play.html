SETTINGS & RULES

Play a Cell card to add your cells to the shared Core, removing enemy cells first. You win when your total reaches the mode target.

Protect: Spend your turn to arm one Protect. It completely blocks the opponent's next shot. Each side starts with 3 stored Protects.

Protect draw: You also reveal a bonus normal card when you arm Protect. Replace one of your three normal cards or discard it.

Cell Pieces: Earn Cell Pieces only when a successful shot removes cells already controlled by your opponent. Adding friendly cells earns none; fully blocked shots earn none. You can also convert a normal Cell Card into its face-value pieces using its top-corner control. Spend 10 pieces on Gold +4 or manually restore a stored Protect for 12 pieces. Conversion and restoration do not end your turn.

Card odds: +1 28%, +2 34%, +3 33%, +4 5%. Gold +4 is reserved in the fourth slot when available.
